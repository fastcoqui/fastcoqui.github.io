What's New In Tesla World

Snapshot checked Oct 19, 2026 — software, FSD and Model Y news Tito is actually talking about, with sources linked on every story.

Software · 2026.38.5Rolled out Oct 10–13, 2026

FSD v14.3.12 lands — 20% faster reactions + Halloween Mode

Tesla's 2026.38.5 update pairs FSD (Supervised) v14.3.12 for HW4/AI4 cars with FSD v14.3 Lite for eligible HW3 cars — plus a seasonal Halloween package.

  • Rewritten AI runtime (MLIR) — ~20% faster reaction time
  • Upgraded vision encoder for low-visibility & rare scenes
  • Less lane bias and tailgating; smarter parking-spot picks
  • Halloween Mode: Ghost Costume, Trick-or-Treat sounds & light flicker, scheduled haunting light show, Photobooth

Sources: Tesla Canada Group · Tesla Robotaxi

FSD · HW3Announced Oct 14, 2026

FSD v14 Lite finally reaches legacy Model S & X

Intel-era (MCU2) Hardware 3 Model S and X get a v14 branch for the first time, as 2026.38.5.1 — closing the last HW3 gap Tesla had tracked for weeks.

  • Distilled HW4 v14 intelligence tuned for HW3 hardware
  • Better merges, pedestrians, traffic lights & cut-ins
  • Speed Profiles + an FSD streak counter included
  • Arrival Options & FSD reversing stay HW3 3/Y-only for now

Sources: Not a Tesla App · TeslAnt changelog

Model YOct 17, 2026

Model Y L gets Acceleration Boost — 0–100 km/h in 4.5s

The long-wheelbase, six-seat Model Y L shaves half a second off its sprint with a free performance upgrade for cars bought from Oct 1, 2026 — with the claimed 681 km WLTP range unchanged.

  • Dual-motor AWD, up to 250 kW DC fast charging
  • Adds up to ~288 km in 15 minutes on a Supercharger

Source: Autocar India

Model Y PerformanceOrders open Oct 1, 2026

2027 Model Y Performance: price cut + new Glacier Blue

Australia's MY27 Performance drops $4,500 to $84,900 before on-roads as production switches to Shanghai — and gains the Glacier Blue finish closest yet to Tito's own blue.

  • New Zen Grey interior option joins the range
  • Premium Long Range AWD also gets quicker: 4.8s → 4.3s

Source: CarExpert
